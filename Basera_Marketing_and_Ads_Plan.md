# Basera — Marketing & Paid Advertising Plan

*Prepared August 22, 2026*

This complements `Basera_Social_Media_Marketing_Plan.md` (organic content calendar, platform strategy, daily Claude prompts) with a paid-advertising plan and messaging for Hostel Groups, roommate visibility, and the Hotels & Guest Houses vertical.

## 1. Paid Advertising Cost Benchmarks (Pakistan, 2026)

Meta (Facebook/Instagram) remains the most cost-efficient paid channel in Pakistan: CPM typically runs **$0.80–$1.50**, among the cheapest in Asia, with Facebook traffic-campaign CPC around **PKR 140–250** (lead-generation campaigns run higher, roughly **PKR 281–845 per lead**). Instagram CPCs and CPMs run consistently higher than Facebook's (Instagram CPM roughly 20–40% above Facebook's in the same market). Costs rise in Q4 (October–December) and are cheapest in Q1 (January–March).

*Sources: [Facebook Ads in Pakistan: How Much Do They Cost — NextSol](https://www.nextsol.pk/facebook-ads-cost-pakistan-2026/), [Meta Ads CPM & CPC Benchmarks by Country 2026 — Adamigo](https://www.adamigo.ai/blog/meta-ads-cpm-cpc-benchmarks-by-country-2026), [Facebook, Instagram & TikTok Ads Cost in Pakistan 2026 — Unboxed](https://unboxed.global/social-media-ads-cost-pakistan/)*

## 2. Funnel & Channel Roles

| Stage | Goal | Primary channel | Ad format |
|---|---|---|---|
| Awareness | Get "verified hostel booking" into consideration | Facebook, TikTok | Video/Reels, broad + lookalike targeting |
| Consideration | Drive traffic to real listings | Instagram, Google Search | Carousel/collection ads, Search ads |
| Conversion | Start the booking | Instagram/Facebook retargeting | Dynamic listing ads, lead-gen forms |
| Retention | Renewals, referrals, rewards | WhatsApp, email/push | Owned channels, not paid |

## 3. Campaign Structure

**Campaign 1 — Brand/Trust Awareness.** Video views + reach. Creative: escrow protection, verification badges, roommate visibility ("Know who you're rooming with before you book"). Broad 18–26 Pakistan audience, layered with a lookalike once pixel data exists.

**Campaign 2 — City/University Conversion.** Traffic/conversions, one ad set per major city. Dynamic creative pulling real listing photos/prices, geo-targeted ~15km of each major university, admissions-season custom audiences (July–Sept, Jan–Feb).

**Campaign 3 — Hostel Groups & Host Acquisition (B2B).** Lead generation targeting multi-property hostel/PG owners ("List your whole chain under one verified brand on Basera"). LinkedIn + Facebook, property-management interest groups. Underinvested lever in most hostel-marketplace plans.

**Campaign 4 — Hotels & Guest Houses (new vertical).** Separate creative from student campaigns — travel/lifestyle framing (Murree, Naran), 25–45 audience, seasonal push before summer/winter tourist seasons. Google Search ads on "guest house Murree booking" likely outperform social here.

**Campaign 5 — Retargeting.** Site visitors who viewed a listing but didn't book (7/14/30-day windows), dynamic ads showing the specific listing plus a light incentive.

## 4. Suggested Monthly Budget Allocation (illustrative)

| Campaign | % of budget | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| Brand/Trust Awareness | 20% | Builds the retargeting pools |
| City/University Conversion | 40% | Core demand generation |
| Hostel Groups & Host Acquisition | 15% | Supply-side, higher value per lead |
| Hotels & Guest Houses launch | 15% | New vertical, needs its own proof period |
| Retargeting | 10% | Small audience, high conversion |

Prioritize City/University Conversion and Retargeting in month one; layer in the rest as budget/data grows (Meta wants ~50 conversions/ad set/week to exit learning phase).

## 5. Creative Angles by Feature

- **Escrow protection:** "Your rent is protected until you move in — not before."
- **Roommate visibility (new):** "See who you'll be living with — before you book." Lead with this; it's a genuine differentiator.
- **Hostel Groups (new):** B2B — "One verified brand, every branch." B2C — group badge next to listings as a trust signal.
- **Hotels & Guest Houses (new):** Lifestyle-first creative, not feature-first — this audience buys the destination.
- **AI Concierge:** "Ask Basera anything about your move — get a real answer in seconds." Good retargeting hook for bouncers.

## 6. Measurement

Track cost-per-booking as the north-star metric. Install Meta Pixel + Google Ads conversion tracking on the booking-confirmation step, pass UTM parameters into the existing referral/loyalty tracking to compare paid-acquired vs. organic/referral retention. Weekly review for month one, then monthly.
