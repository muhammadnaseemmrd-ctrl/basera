The Problem

Finding safe student housing in Pakistan is still informal

  • Room hunting happens mostly through Facebook groups, WhatsApp forwards, and word of mouth.
  • No standard way to verify a hostel/host before sending money.
  • Parents have little visibility once a student moves in.
  • Rent is usually paid in cash or direct transfer — no protection if a room doesn't match what was promised.
  • Price comparison across cities/universities is manual and time-consuming.
  • Disputes over deposits have no neutral third party.
